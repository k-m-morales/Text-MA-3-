Hand Drawn Letters

Due date: February 1

Requirements

  • Choose three typefaces.
  • Draw all required uppercase letters: B, Q, K, Y.
  • Draw lowercase a, g, p, y, and one additional vowel.
  • Draw one numeral of your choice.
  • Use all required sizes: 36, 72, 222, 450 points and 25 picas.
  • Write a two-sentence biography of each selected typeface designer.
  • Use one color only, excluding the paper color.

Typeface Options

Bodoni Extra Bold Italic, Trade Gothic Condensed 20 Bold, Baskerville Italic, Garamond Roman, Interstate Gothic, Futura Extra Bold.

This assignment is to be performed during the recitation, and also where ever the student is most comfortable. During the recitation the LA will help guide research for the student group and general solutions for tools and expectations.

There are 150 Drawings which can be made into any artifact the student would like, a book, a poster, a stack of uncoordinated papers. Whatever.

Grading guidance: Completing the work earns a B-level result; A-level work makes it interesting.

Numbers & Statistics

Due date: February 22

Pull 1 Statistic from each of the following:

  • Financial Times — financial technology
  • New York Times — opinion/editorial
  • ESPN — sports

Provide citations, including a photo citation for the ESPN source.

What to Create

  • Three separate slide decks.
  • Four slides in each deck.
  • Recreate an appropriate branded template for each author.
  • Use three different perspectives: pro, con, and neutral.
  • This is an assignment at the intersection of speculative futures and storytelling using—more or less—accepted forms of source material and retold and cited for the purposes of promoting a favorable/unfavorable circumstance and the consequences that are in the truth of this "fact". Numbers are central to this work. There must be a number story..
  • Upload completed work to Canvas.

AI warning: Use AI at your own risk. AI errors are common, if the AI errors are present in your finished work. Your final grade for the assignment will drop by a full letter grade.

Example: A 4 Slide deck produced from a piece of information found in an ESPN report about the inbound trend of "Leisurewear". The statistic might support investment in a "breathable textile startup". The author might be a tech/textile company selling the investment opportunity. Located in Jaipur, Northern India. With more than one technical fabric in its portfolio.

03 / GRADING

How is your grade calculated?

Major Assignments

30% of final grade

Three assignments. Late penalty: 10%.

Minor Assignments

50% of final grade

10 accepted out of 15 available.

No late assignments accepted.

Attendance, Participation & Extra Credit

20% of final grade

Two extra-credit assignments can substitute for one minor assignment or one absence.

04 / COURSE POLICIES

What should you know?

Attendance:

Much like other purchases—you will find yourself responsible for the quality of your experience. My responsibility is to many students—I expect that if you want to know what this whole typography thing is about, you're going to show up on time—all the time. If you think this might not be the right time for this class consider doing this another time. If you are found absent more than twice you will be ineligible for an A grade. More than 3 times you would be ineligible for a C.

At Recitations:

You will pick up an assignment that you can perform during that period. Sign and place the assignment in the folder on your way out of the room. Each recitation assignment is also an attendance mechanism

If the recitation has a reading and discussion assignment. This Happens during the session and the text is read out loud with everyone present. Each person reads one or two paragraphs. Each person writes a 2 sentence synopsis by hand.

Each recitation has a practice which you can participate with as long as you please. If it is a quiz the quiz can rerefer to any source. All quizzes are to be handed in before you leave. The work you make must be handed in before you leave the room.

There are no late assignments for the recitations don't ask for extensions due to absence. 10 Assignments will be accepted of a possible 15 assignments during the semester.

Final exam: None.
