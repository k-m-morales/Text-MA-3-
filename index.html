03 / MAJOR ASSIGNMENTS

Let's make something.

Complete three major assignments throughout the semester. Click each assignment to see its requirements.

Hand Drawn Letters

Original due date: September 14

Explore typography by accurately drawing letterforms in three different typefaces.

Requirements

  • Choose three typefaces.
  • Draw all required uppercase letters: B, Q, K, Y.
  • Draw lowercase a, g, p, y, and one additional vowel.
  • Draw one numeral of your choice.
  • Use all required sizes: 36, 72, 222, 450 points and 25 picas.
  • Write a two-sentence biography of each selected typeface designer.
  • Use one color only, excluding the paper color.

Typeface Options

Bodoni Extra Bold Italic, Trade Gothic Condensed 20 Bold, Baskerville Italic, Garamond Roman, Interstate Gothic, Futura Extra Bold.

Work during recitation or wherever you are comfortable. Learning assistants can help with research, tools, and expectations.

The assignment involves 150 drawings, which may be presented as a book, poster, or another format.

Grading guidance: Completing the work earns a B-level result; A-level work makes it interesting.

Numbers & Statistics

Original due date: October 5

Research statistics and use them to tell compelling visual stories.

Research Sources

  • Financial Times — financial technology
  • New York Times — opinion/editorial
  • ESPN — sports

Collect one statistic from each source. Provide citations, including a photo citation for the ESPN source.

What to Create

  • Three separate slide decks.
  • Four slides in each deck.
  • Recreate an appropriate branded template for each author.
  • Use three different perspectives: pro, con, and neutral.
  • Build a factual story centered around numbers.
  • Support inferences with evidence.
  • Upload completed work to Canvas.

AI warning: Errors introduced by AI in the finished work result in a full letter-grade reduction.

Original example: A four-slide ESPN-based deck about athleisurewear trends could present an investment opportunity in a breathable-textile startup in Jaipur, India, supported by factual research.

04 / GRADING

How is your grade calculated?

Major Assignments

30% of final grade

Three assignments. Late penalty: 10%.

Minor Assignments

50% of final grade

10 accepted out of 15 available.

No late assignments accepted.

Attendance, Participation & Extra Credit

20% of final grade

Two extra-credit assignments can substitute for one minor assignment or one absence.

05 / COURSE POLICIES

What should you know?

Attendance

Arrive on time and attend consistently. More than two absences makes you ineligible for an A. More than three makes you ineligible for a C.

Recitations

Learning assistants provide course summaries, handouts, and activities. Complete and submit recitation work before leaving the room.

Reading & Discussion

Students may read one or two paragraphs aloud and write a two-sentence handwritten synopsis.

Quizzes & Deadlines

Quizzes may refer to any course source. Submit quizzes and recitation assignments before leaving. No late recitation assignments or absence-based extensions are accepted.

Recitation activities also serve as attendance records. Practice activities may be continued as long as desired.

There are 15 possible recitation assignments, of which 10 are accepted.

Final exam: None.
